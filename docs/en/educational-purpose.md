# 🎓 Educational Purpose

Syllabus is designed to:

* Replace three separate, partially redundant documents (an internal plan, a student-facing
  plan without answer keys, and a tutor plan with grading guidance) with one single source of
  data, removing the real risk of a field being corrected in one copy and forgotten in another
* Formalise, inside Moodle itself, a coordination approval step that today happens by e-mail or
  loose file, outside the platform and without any audit trail
* Guarantee that tutors and students always see the version coordination actually approved,
  never an outdated copy that drifted from what was reviewed
* Let the teacher work in one autosaving form instead of retyping the same content three times

Suitable for:

* Any institution that requires coordination sign-off on a course's syllabus/lesson plan before
  it reaches students and tutors
* Courses where tutors need grading criteria and answer keys the student-facing view must never
  expose
* Programmes that want a single, always-current syllabus document tied to the actual Moodle
  course it describes, instead of a static file attached elsewhere
